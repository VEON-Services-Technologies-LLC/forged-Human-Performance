Introduction to ForgedHP Transformation Calculators

Welcome to the ForgedHP Transformation Calculators page! This powerful tool is designed to help you on your fitness journey by providing essential data to track your progress and set clear, realistic goals. Using these calculators, you'll be able to estimate vital statistics like your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR), Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE), lean mass, fat mass, and more. These insights will guide you in creating a personalized approach to transform your body, tailored specifically to your current condition and future goals.

Fat Loss Calculator

At ForgedHP, we believe in data-driven transformation. The calculators on this page are here to offer you a clear snapshot of where you are now and how to reach your desired body composition.

How This Calculator Works

This calculator will help you:

  • Estimate your current body fat percentage based on visual models
  • Calculate your BMR—the number of calories your body needs at rest
  • Determine your TDEE based on your activity level
  • Analyze your current and target fat mass and lean mass

Keep in mind that your body is unique and it may not look exactly like one of the examples. This estimation helps get your transformation journey started. We'll dial everything in over time!
