Daily Caloric Intake Calculator

Get personalized calorie recommendations based on your specific fitness goals - whether you want to lose fat, gain muscle, recompose your body, or maintain your current physique.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select Your Primary Goal: Choose one of the four options that matches your fitness objective.
  2. Enter Your Details: Input your TDEE (from the Fat Loss Calculator) and any required goal-specific metrics.
  3. Press Calculate: Get your customized calorie recommendations for different change levels.

Understanding Your Options

Fat Loss

Creates a caloric deficit to help you burn fat while preserving lean muscle mass.

Muscle Gain

Provides a caloric surplus to fuel muscle growth and recovery.

Body Recomposition

Balances calories to simultaneously lose fat and gain muscle.

Maintenance

Helps you maintain your current weight and body composition.

Macro Ratio Calculator

Break down your daily calories into precise grams of protein, carbs, and fats to perfectly align your nutrition with your fitness goals.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter Your Caloric Intake: Input your daily calorie target (from the previous calculators).
  2. Set Your Macro Percentages: Adjust the sliders to your preferred protein, carbs, and fat ratios.
  3. Press Calculate: Get your daily macronutrient breakdown in grams.

Macronutrient Guidelines

Protein

Essential for muscle growth and recovery. Recommended: 25-40% of calories (0.7-1g per lb of body weight).

Carbohydrates

Primary energy source for training. Recommended: 40-55% of calories.

Fats

Supports hormone production and health. Recommended: 20-35% of calories.
