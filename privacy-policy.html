Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Forged Human Performance ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information in connection with our website (forgedhp.com), mobile applications, and services (collectively, the "Services").

We comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), and are committed to handling your personal data responsibly. Please read this policy carefully to understand our privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Account Information: Username, password, and account preferences
  • Health and Fitness Data: Age, weight, height, fitness goals, medical history, dietary restrictions, exercise preferences, and health conditions
  • Payment Information: Credit card details and billing address (processed through secure payment processors)
  • Communication Data: Messages, questions, feedback, and correspondence with our coaches and support team
  • Discovery Call Information: Additional information shared during consultation calls or sessions

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, content accessed, time spent, and interactions with our Services
  • Location Information: General location based on IP address (not precise GPS tracking)
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies to track usage patterns and preferences

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Payment Processors: Transaction information from payment processors
  • Analytics Providers: Usage data from Google Analytics and similar services
  • Social Media: If you connect your social media account to our Services, we may collect publicly available information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our coaching services and support
  • Personalization: To tailor coaching programs to your specific needs, goals, and health data
  • Communication: To send you updates, newsletters, promotional content, and respond to inquiries
  • Payment Processing: To process transactions and manage billing and subscriptions
  • Analytics and Improvement: To understand how you use our Services and improve our offerings
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal obligations
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, and technical problems
  • Health and Safety: To ensure appropriate coaching and exercise recommendations based on your health status

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party vendors who assist us in delivering our Services (payment processors, email providers, analytics services)
  • Our Coaching Team: With our coaches and support staff as necessary to provide personalized coaching services
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to comply with legal processes
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our legal rights, privacy, safety, or that of our users
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or sale of assets

4.2 Sensitive Data and Health Information

We treat health and fitness data with heightened care. We will not use or disclose sensitive health information without your explicit prior consent, except as necessary to provide our coaching services. Your health data is used solely to create personalized coaching plans and provide appropriate guidance.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Secure payment processing through PCI-compliant processors
  • Access controls restricting data access to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Secure data backup and recovery procedures

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use our Services at your own risk.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) Rights

Under the TDPSA, Texas consumers have the following rights:

  • Right to Know: You may request what personal data we have collected about you
  • Right to Access: You may request access to the personal data we maintain about you
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal data we have collected (subject to certain exceptions)
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data and targeted advertising
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at matt@forgedhp.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 45 days of receiving your request.

6.2 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails or by contacting us directly.

6.3 Cookies and Tracking

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect your ability to use certain features of our Services.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Health and fitness data is retained for the duration of your membership or coaching relationship, plus a reasonable period afterward (typically 3 years) to maintain records and comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to our legal and business obligations to retain certain data.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services before providing your personal information.

10. International Users

Our Services are based in the United States. If you access our Services from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States. By using our Services, you consent to this transfer.

11. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). For more information about your California privacy rights, please contact us at matt@forgedhp.com.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, and other factors. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and, if the changes are significant, by providing additional notice (such as emailing you or posting a notice on our website).

Your continued use of our Services after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
