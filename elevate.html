Elevate: 6-Week Challenge

Your 6-Week Habit Transformation Journey.

Welcome to the Elevate 6-Week Challenge! This is your first step toward a stronger, healthier, and more fulfilled life. In just six weeks, we'll help you integrate the key habits that fuel your body, sharpen your mind, and elevate your daily living. Transformation is built on progress, not perfection.

Master the 5 Pillars of Elevated Performance

  • Move Your Body with Purpose: The goal is to move intentionally at least 5 times per week.
  • Eat for Performance: Fuel your body by prioritizing protein and whole foods every day.
  • Hydrate for Health: Proper hydration is fundamental. The goal is to drink at least half your body weight in ounces of water daily.
  • Prioritize Recovery: Get 7-9 hours of quality sleep and spend at least 20 minutes in natural sunlight daily.
  • Build Your Mindset: Spend time daily nurturing your mind and spirit through reading, prayer or meditation, and gratitude journaling.
