Frequently Asked Questions

+

Who is this group for?

This group is for coaches, athletes, leaders, entrepreneurs, and anyone ready to make a leap in their mental game through proven curriculum and peer support.

+

Do I need a gym membership?

No. We’ll build your program around your preferred environment and available equipment.

+

Do I need prior experience with mindset training or performance psychology?

No prior experience required—the curriculum and coaching are designed to guide you from the ground up, whether you’re a beginner or seasoned student.

+

How often do we meet?

Virtual meet-ups are held bi-weekly over six months, creating consistent momentum and space for growth.

+

What is the group size?

Groups are intentionally kept small to foster connection, accountability, and tailored discussion. Usually between 5-10 members.

+

What if I miss a session?

All sessions are recorded and available for review, so you won’t fall behind if you have to miss a meeting.

+

Will I get individual attention?

Absolutely—while the power is in the group dynamic, coaches provide personal feedback and guidance throughout the program.

+

Is this program confidential?

Yes, we emphasize privacy and trust. What’s shared in the group stays in the group.

+

Can these skills be applied to work, sport, or personal life?

Yes! The skills and tools you’ll master are designed for real-world use across all areas of performance and wellbeing.
