Calculate Your Running Pace

Enter your recent run data to get your current pace, training zone recommendations (based on Jack Daniels' VDOT methodology), and race time predictions using the Riegel formula.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select Distance: Choose the distance of your recent run (1 mile to marathon).
  2. Enter Your Time: Input hours, minutes, and seconds.
  3. Enter RPE (Optional): Rate your effort on a 1-10 scale for more accurate predictions.
  4. Press Calculate: Get your pace, training zones, and race predictions.

Understanding RPE for Running

RPE 9-10 (Race Effort)

All-out effort - use for race predictions

RPE 7-8 (Hard Workout)

Tempo/threshold - predictions less precise

RPE 1-6 (Training Run)

Easy/moderate - no race predictions shown

RPE 9-10 gives most accurate race predictions
