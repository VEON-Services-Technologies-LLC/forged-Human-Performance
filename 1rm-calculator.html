Calculate Your 1RM

This calculator uses three proven formulas (Epley, Brzycki, and RPE-based) and averages them for the most accurate estimation of your one-rep max.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the Weight: Input the weight you lifted (in lbs - stay consistent).
  2. Enter Reps Performed: How many reps did you complete? (1-12 recommended for accuracy)
  3. Enter RPE: Rate of Perceived Exertion on a scale of 6-10, where 10 = absolute max effort.
  4. Press Calculate: Get your estimated 1RM and complete percentage table instantly.

Understanding RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)

RPE 10

Maximum effort - could not do another rep

RPE 9

Could do 1 more rep

RPE 8

Could do 2 more reps

RPE 7

Could do 3 more reps

RPE 6

Could do 4+ more reps
