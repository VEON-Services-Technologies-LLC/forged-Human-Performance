Courtney Carreon

Human Performance Coach

Courtney is a CSCS with over six years of experience designing and coaching training programs for a wide range of athletes and everyday performers, including tactical and adaptive athletes, older adults, and pre‑ and postpartum mothers. She is currently pursuing a graduate degree in Sport and Human Performance.

Her athletic background started in competitive gymnastics and continued through collegiate cheerleading. Her professional path was further shaped by firsthand exposure to the Naval Special Warfare pipeline during her husband's time in the SEAL Teams.

Bo Patton

Tactical Performance Coach

Bo is a Texas Peace Officer with over a decade of experience in patrol, training, and tactical operations, including service as an ERT (SWAT) Team Leader. A competitive USPSA shooter and Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu purple belt with a background in Muay Thai, Sambo, and wrestling.

At ForgedHP, Bo's mission is to develop capable, confident shooters and fighters who can think clearly and act decisively when it matters most. His coaching blends technical skill development, scenario-based training, and efficient tactics that carry over directly to real-world performance.
