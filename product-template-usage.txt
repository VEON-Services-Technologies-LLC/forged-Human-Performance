For each new product, duplicate this template and replace these placeholder values:

PRODUCT_DESCRIPTION - Meta description for SEO
PRODUCT_NAME - Name of your product
PRODUCT_IMAGE_URL - URL for social media sharing image
PRODUCT_PAGE_URL - Canonical URL of the product page
PRODUCT_HERO_TITLE - Main headline in the hero section
PRODUCT_HERO_SUBTITLE - Subtitle in the hero section
PRODUCT_INTRO_TEXT - Introductory paragraph
PRODUCT_MAIN_IMAGE - Path to the main product image
PRODUCT_PRICE - Price information
GUARANTEE_DETAILS - Details about your guarantee
UPGRADE_OPTION - Information about upgrade options
PAYMENT_TERMS - Payment terms (e.g., "Cancel anytime")
PRODUCT_BENEFITS_DESCRIPTION - Description of product benefits
FEATURE_X_TITLE and FEATURE_X_DESCRIPTION - Product features
PRODUCT_PURCHASE_LINK - Link to purchase the product
ADD_TO_CART_BUTTON_TEXT - Text for the call-to-action button
FAQ_QUESTION_X and FAQ_ANSWER_X - Frequently asked questions
FINAL_CTA_TEXT - Final call-to-action text
PAYMENT_TERMS_NOTE - Additional note about payment terms
